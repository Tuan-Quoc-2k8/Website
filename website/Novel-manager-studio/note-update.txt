# Book Reader UI & Navigation Redesign

The exported Book HTML currently functions correctly, but the reading experience is poor for long novels.

Current problems:

* All chapters are merged into one extremely long page.
* Readers must scroll continuously through dozens or hundreds of chapters.
* Table of Contents becomes difficult to use when chapter count is large.
* The interface feels like a raw document rather than a real novel website.
* Navigation becomes slow and tiring for long stories.

Please redesign the exported Book system to behave like a modern online novel reader.

---

# 2. Chapter Separation System

Current issue:

All chapters are rendered into one giant page.

This must be removed.

---

Required:

Each chapter should become an independent page/view.

Example:

```text
/index.html
/chapter-001.html
/chapter-002.html
/chapter-003.html
...
```

or

```text
index.html?page=chapter001
```

Either solution is acceptable.

---

Benefits:

* Faster loading
* Easier navigation
* Better reading experience
* Scales to hundreds of chapters

---

# 4. Collapsible Chapter List

For novels with many chapters:

Current chapter list becomes extremely long.

Required:

Automatically group chapters.

Example:

```text
▼ Chương 1 - 20

▼ Chương 21 - 40

▶ Chương 41 - 60

▶ Chương 61 - 80
```

Only expanded groups show chapter names.

This dramatically reduces scrolling.

---

Alternative:

Add chapter search.

Example:

```text
🔍 Tìm chương...
```

Typing:

```text
120
```

Immediately jumps to:

```text
Chương 120
```

# 7. Chapter Reader Optimization

Reader should only load:

* current chapter
* previous chapter
* next chapter

Do NOT load the entire novel at once.

This improves performance for:

* 100+ chapters
* large projects
* image-heavy novels

# Design Goal

Transform the exported Book from a long HTML document into a complete novel-reading website.

The experience should resemble modern online novel platforms, where:

* The novel has a homepage.
* Chapters are separated.
* Navigation is fast.
* Readers never need to scroll through hundreds of chapters.
* The interface remains lightweight and works completely offline.
